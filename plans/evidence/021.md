## Implementation and focused parity

`prepareWorldRenderSnapshot(world, manifest, viewport)` now:

- builds a manifest-identity animation cache and fresh viewport-local
  `unitById`, `researchByBuildingId`, and `pendingAttackBySourceId` maps;
- preserves legacy first-match behavior with first-write-wins insertion;
- combines unit indexing and culling in one source pass, culls every owned
  entity list before its single stable sort, and leaves source arrays unchanged;
- preserves exact unit/corpse/effect comparators and projectile equal-level
  source order;
- partitions sorted corpse/projectile/effect lists once at draw level 40; and
- publishes resettable `plan021.renderPreparation.*` diagnostics outside
  world/save state.

The active viewport and every split viewport prepare independent snapshots.
`renderWorld.ts` consumes the prepared lists and maps without importing Plan
020, retaining Pixi objects, changing draw call order, or replacing tracked
constructors/destructors.

The focused verifier covers:

- hidden construction, invisible utility, resource-contained, fogged,
  offscreen, edge, and parabolic-projectile fixtures;
- stable equal-key order and exact level-39/40 strata;
- independent viewport snapshots, manifest-identity cache reuse, fresh dynamic
  indexes, source-array immutability, and diagnostic reset/counts;
- duplicate unit, research, pending-attack, and animation keys selecting the
  same first record as legacy `.find(...)`;
- downstream attack/research/corpse/last-seen animation action and frame
  selection; and
- the unchanged Plan 018 tracked call-site footprint: 5 containers,
  12 graphics, 12 sprites, 2 texts, and 2 tracked destroy calls.
